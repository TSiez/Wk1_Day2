Lens 01 / Smart Glasses

A quiet HUD
for the face.

Lens 01 projects a single-colour overlay onto a 26 degree field. Navigation, messages, biometrics — a layer of context without the weight of a screen.

Display

26 degree waveguide — one colour

A silicon photonics waveguide in prescription-compatible frames. The overlay is monochromatic by design — high contrast, low cognitive load.

Sensors

Eye-tracking and ambient

Infrared eye-tracking for foveated rendering. Ambient light, accelerometer and temperature sensors. Fully integrated with the Tester Mesh health layer.

Endurance

Eight hours active

A solid-state micro-cell behind the right temple charges in forty minutes via the included dock. Prescription lenses available through partner opticians.
