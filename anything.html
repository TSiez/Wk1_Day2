What you’re scrolling through above is the Loop Ring exploded into the five components that make it work: a brushed grade-5 titanium outer shell, a polished mirror inner band, a miniature PCB with gold traces and a tiny SoC, a curved silicon-anode battery cell, and an inward-facing sensor stack of six green / red / infrared photodiodes.

Eight seconds, one continuous transition — rendered through Veo 3 from two hand-composed bookend stills. The bench was dressed black on black; the only colour you’ll see is whatever the titanium decides to throw back.
