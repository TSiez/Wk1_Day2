Common Questions / 06

If you’re wondering.

The questions we hear most often. Don’t see yours? Email us — a real person writes back, usually within a day.

What exactly is the “Tester mesh”?
A small, locally-routed radio protocol that lets every Tester device talk to every other Tester device without leaving your home. Devices negotiate amongst themselves — the watch can hand off a call to Echo Field; the lock can ask the watch if it’s really you. No cloud round-trip is required for any of it.
Do I need every device for the mesh to work?
No. Each device is fully functional on its own. The mesh just becomes more useful as you add more pieces — the same way a good record collection works. Most people start with the Watch Pro and one other piece, usually the Lamp or the Field.
How does the privacy ledger actually work?
Every device action — a voice request, a sensor read, an automation — is signed and journaled to a local ledger on the Hub Display (or your phone if you don’t have one). You can open it, read it line by line, and revoke anything. We can’t see any of it. Nothing leaves your home unless you explicitly opt in.
What’s the warranty and repair story?
Two-year warranty on everything. Every device is designed to be opened — the Watch Pro and the Ring share a common battery cell we’ll swap for €39. We publish service manuals for anyone who wants to do it themselves.
Where do you ship and when?
EU, UK, US, Canada, Japan, Australia, and Singapore at launch. Edition 01 of the Watch Pro ships in late Q3 2026 — numbered to the first hundred. The rest of the family follows on a rolling cadence through 2026 and 2027.
What happens if Tester stops existing?
Honest question, honest answer: the protocol is open. Every Tester device can run firmware that we’ve already published under a permissive license. If we vanish, the mesh keeps working, and another studio can ship replacements. We don’t plan on vanishing — but we built the family so that you don’t have to take our word for that.
