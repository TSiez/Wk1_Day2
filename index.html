Section / 01

Engineered like an instrument,
not an accessory.

Six things we obsessed over. The rest is downstream of these.

  1. 01

    Sapphire optic stack

    A six-channel photodiode array behind aerospace sapphire. Reads pulse, oxygen and skin temperature without losing accuracy under sweat or sun.

  2. 02

    Six-axis IMU

    Gyroscope and accelerometer fused at 1 kHz. Counts a stair, a stride or a swing. Knows the difference between a wave and a wrist-flick.

  3. 03

    Dual-band GNSS

    L1 + L5 multi-constellation positioning. Cleaner fixes in urban canyons and deep cover. Track in metres, not minutes.

  4. 04

    14-day endurance

    An LTPO display that drops to 1 Hz at rest, a sub-microamp coprocessor and a silicon-anode cell. Two weeks of ordinary use. Forty hours of full-fat GPS.

  5. 05

    200 m water resistance

    Pressure-tested to twenty atmospheres. Open-water swim, freediving, white-water — the Pro logs through all of it without a case change.

  6. 06

    AMOLED 1.4″ LTPO

    A 466×466 always-on panel that dims to a glance and pushes 2,000 nits in direct sun. Sapphire crystal up top, of course.

Section / 02 — Inside the Pro

Eighty-four components,
twelve millimetres thick.

We took the Pro apart on camera so you could see what your wrist will be wearing. Every part chosen for a single, stubborn reason.

A Bezel Brushed Grade-5 Titanium Machined from a single billet. 40% lighter than steel, 2× the yield strength. B Crystal 2.5 mm Sapphire Anti-reflective coated on both faces. Mohs 9 — scratches with diamond, not life. C Sensor block Six-channel PPG Green, red, infrared and UV diodes tuned for skin tones I through VI.
